## Community (Free)

The Community edition is free for development and testing. It supports clusters of up to **3 nodes** or **60 pods** and requires internet connectivity. Download a license key from the [License Portal](https://console.kubeshark.com) and set it as a Helm value.

> To get started with the Community edition, [install **Kubeshark**](/en/install).

Production use means any license other than Community. See [Community and Enterprise](/en/plans) and the [pricing page](https://kubeshark.com/pricing).

## Enterprise

Enterprise differs from Community in four ways: size (no Community limits), air-gapped support (no internet connectivity required), SSO, authorization, and sensitive data redaction, and support.

Enterprise pricing does not depend on cluster size. It is based on the number of pods you target (pods of interest). Smaller packages cost less, so the budget stays predictable and you control the spend.

**Predictable budget, with spend you control.**

Request a quote at [get started](https://kubeshark.com/get-started).

### Obtaining an Enterprise POC License

To obtain an Enterprise POC license, you'll first need an account in the [License Portal](https://console.kubeshark.com/), which is used to manage licenses. A complimentary license is provisioned upon sign-up.

Once your account is created, request a license at [get started](https://kubeshark.com/get-started), and provide the email address used to create the License Portal account. We'll convert your complimentary license to an Enterprise license and notify you once it's ready.

To retrieve your license, log in to the [License Portal](https://console.kubeshark.com/) and download your license key.
