Kubeshark has two offerings: **Community** (free) and **Enterprise**. There is no public price list. The comparison is on the [pricing page](https://kubeshark.com/pricing).

Production use means any license other than Community.

## Community (Free)

Community is free for development and testing:

- Clusters of up to **3 nodes** or **60 pods**
- Requires internet connectivity
- No SSO, authorization, or sensitive data redaction
- Community support
- Download a license key from the [License Portal](https://console.kubeshark.com) and set it as a Helm value

## Enterprise

Enterprise differs from Community in four ways:

- **Size** — no Community limits. Pricing does not depend on cluster size. It is based on the number of pods you target (pods of interest). Smaller packages cost less, so the budget stays predictable and you control the spend.
- **Air-gapped support** — no internet connectivity required
- **SSO, authorization, and sensitive data redaction**
- **Support** — dedicated support (Slack, Zoom, and premium onboarding)

**Predictable budget, with spend you control.**

Request a quote at [get started](https://kubeshark.com/get-started).

## Feature Comparison

| | Community | Enterprise |
|---|---|---|
| Size | Up to 3 nodes or 60 pods | Any cluster size, sized by the pods you target |
| Air-gapped support | No (internet required) | Yes (no internet connectivity required) |
| SSO, authorization, and sensitive data redaction | No | Yes |
| Support | Community | Dedicated |

[Install Community](/en/install) or [start with Enterprise](https://kubeshark.com/get-started).
